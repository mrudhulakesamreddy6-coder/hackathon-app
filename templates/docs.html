Abstract & Executive Summary

DisasterSync AI represents an Agentic AI platform designed to maximize efficiency and situational awareness when responding to large-scale disasters (floods, fires, earthquakes, and mass accidents). The technology gathers reports from citizens and emergency channels, normalizes them, verifies spatial coordinates, clusters duplicate calls, and coordinates specialized AI agents (Coordinator, Police, Fire & Rescue, Medical, and Resource Management). An AI coordinator synthesizes structured action recommendations, but final consequential decisions remain strictly in human hands (Human-in-the-Loop).

Multi-Agent Architecture (Section 7)

Agent Name Core Responsibility Example Structured Output
Coordinator Agent Assigns tasks, combines agent responses, calculates urgency scores (0-100), detects duplicates. Synthesized action plan, priority rating, and task owners.
Police Coordination Agent Public safety, traffic divergence, crowd control, 500m perimeter cordon, evacuation corridors. Police tactical checklist, road blockage guidance.
Monitoring & Duplicate Agent Calculates Haversine spatial distance (<2km) and text similarity to flag duplicate reports. Duplicate cluster alert with parent incident ID and confidence %.
Fire & Rescue Agent Fire containment, water level analysis, flood watercraft, hydraulic cutters, building collapse. Specialized equipment checklist (boats, ladders, cutters).
Medical Assistance Agent Casualty triage classification (Red/Yellow/Green), ambulance demand, hospital ICU bed status. Mass casualty triage alert and trauma bed reservations.
Resource Management Agent Tracks availability of vehicles, boats, beds, teams; calculates proximity and shortages. Asset allocation recommendation & shortage alerts.
Communication Agent Formulates simulated CAD dispatches, digital pagers, radio transmissions, and public bulletins. Standardized CAD dispatch payload and public safety warning.

Operational Workflow & Safety Boundaries

  1. Receive: A citizen submits an incident report or clicks the 1-Click Urgent SOS Panic Button.
  2. Validate: System validates required fields, timestamp, source, GPS location, and runs Haversine spatial duplicate checks.
  3. Assess: Transparent urgency calculation factors in threat to life (+40 SOS, +30 trapped, +25 casualties, hazard type).
  4. Coordinate: Specialized AI agents analyze public safety, fire containment, casualty triage, and asset allocation.
  5. Review (Human-in-the-Loop): Human incident commanders review evidence, certainty levels, and suggested dispatches.
  6. Dispatch & Update: Simulated alerts are authorized; field officers update status (`Reported`, `Verified`, `Dispatched`, `In Progress`, `Resolved`, `Closed`).
  7. Audit: All submissions, AI triage events, and officer approvals are permanently logged in the audit trail.

Hackathon Safety & Ethics Compliance

This project is developed strictly as a technology prototype for the 24Hrs Hackathon on Generative AI. All emergency alerts, dispatch messages, and resource allocations are entirely simulated. The prototype does not contact real emergency services (911/112/100) or dispatch real response personnel.
